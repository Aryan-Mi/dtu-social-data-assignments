The Wait Gap.
Five explorations.

Five different visual treatments of the same NHAMCS analysis (2009–2022, 323,137 visits). Each one tells the gender wait-time story with a different voice and aesthetic.

1.
Refined editorial · improved

Same DNA, smoother breath.

The current story, refined. Better typography (Fraunces + Inter), smoother spring-eased animations, a reading-progress bar, active-nav highlighting, hover micro-interactions, scoped chart lazy-load. Same color story, more polish.

2.
Mystery · withheld until the end

No numbers until you've seen it.

A visual investigation in 5 chapters. Numbers are blurred, hidden behind dots and shapes through the whole story — colors, silhouettes, and an animated mystery heatmap. All values reveal at once in a black "The Numbers" finale.

3.
Clinical · ER monitor

It looks like a vitals panel.

JetBrains Mono, dark medical UI, ECG line that draws, pulsing red alert dot, channel-style live charts. Sections are framed as "channels" with status indicators. A live clock ticks. The conclusion is a "diagnosis card."

4.
Brutalist · field report

A newspaper, slammed into evidence.

Newspaper masthead, Bodoni × Space Mono, ruling lines, drop caps, 3-column lede, oversized stamps, a red "ruling" page near the end. Numbers are exhibits A through F. Built to feel like an investigative print issue.

5.
Cinematic · documentary

Five acts. Letterboxed.

A dark documentary aesthetic. Letterbox bars slide in on scroll. Act tracker pins to top. Gold & crimson accents glow softly. Slow, deliberate reveals, breathing radial light, Fraunces serif overlay. End title card delivers the finding.
