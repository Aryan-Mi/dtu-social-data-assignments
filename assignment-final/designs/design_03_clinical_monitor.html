Final read: the ER is not neutral.

Across pain levels, triage categories, four Census regions, and 14 consecutive years, female sex is associated with a longer time-to-provider. Pattern is robust to all measured covariates.

Finding 01 · Systematic
25/25
Triage × pain cells in which the gap appears in at least one direction. Pattern is non-random.
Finding 02 · Geographic
+2min
Midwest regional gap — twice the national mean. Local protocols and hospital culture amplify the effect.
Finding 03 · Race compounds
+5min
NH Black women at mild pain — single largest deviation from band median observed.
Finding 04 · Clinically material
3min
Window for thrombolysis efficacy in acute ischaemic stroke. Two minutes of delay is not trivial.
